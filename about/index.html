Who built the house

Kenny & Don — two people, one house.

KDK Interests exists because two people stuck together. What started as a handshake on a handful of houses turned into years of building side by side — through renovations, hard seasons, and the slow work of trust that doesn't show up on paper.

This site, and the companies under its roof, are what that partnership grew into: a posture that's global, a standard that's elevated, and a house built to hold more rooms as they're needed.

Why a house

Your house is your home. This one is ours.

A house has a roof, rooms, and the door opens when it should. The future is the horizon. KDK Interests is the roof. KDK TX and KDK Media are two of the rooms in the house. More rooms get built when the work is real enough to deserve a door.

A house is also how we work. Homes for families to live and be happy. Media channels that provide stories with a pulse. We live life as fully as we can — educated, professional, with a global posture. Our light will always shine so you can see who lives here.

KDK Interests is looking for investments and projects that grow the brand and will open the next door. Something worth getting into, and a door worth opening. If that’s you, write us.
